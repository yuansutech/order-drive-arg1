diary-01.txt —— 2008-03-15

March 15, 2008 · Saturday · Clear

An intern came to the station today.

Her name is Xiaoman Lin. Pretty name.

They put her on The Elite Lounge as a floor assistant. I was in the sound booth tuning equipment when she pushed the door open and asked if there were spare headphones.

I said there were. She took a pair, said thanks, and left.

I watched her walk away for a long time.

I don't know why. In that moment I forgot what I was tuning.

— Later I learned March 15 was her first day at the station.

I wrote the date down.

0315.
