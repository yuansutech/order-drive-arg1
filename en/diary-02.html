April 10, 2009 · Friday · Overcast

Xiaoman has been off lately.

She started looking into the South District land. You know — the old factory block next to the station. Demolished in 2003. Empty ever since.

She said it was for a story. But I saw her notebook. Names, amounts, dates. Page after page.

On April 8 she came home very late, looking pale. I asked what was wrong. She said nothing.

On April 9 she stayed at the office until dawn. I texted her. She replied with three words:

"Don't worry."

On April 10 she told me:

"If I'm ever not here, check my blog. You know the password."

I asked what she meant. She smiled and said nothing.

— I keep feeling something is about to happen.
