April 17, 2009 · Friday · Rain

Xiaoman is missing.

Last night she finished recording the final episode and said she had to go to the South District.

I said I'd go with her. She said no, she'd be quick.

She left around 11 pm. It was raining.

At midnight I called. Phone off.

At 1 am I called her roommate. She hadn't come home.

At 3 am I called the police.

By the time they arrived, it was dawn. They asked a few questions, took a statement, and left.

In her drawer I found a photo. The South District factory site.

On the back, a number: 0417

I don't know what it means. Maybe a date. Maybe something else.

I wrote it down.

— This is the last entry.

If I find her, I'll keep writing.

If I don't... this is where it ends.
