// Qingchuan Memory · Personal site · 2008-2009

Before anything else

This is where I keep things.

My name is Mo Shen. I do sound at Qingchuan TV. You wouldn't know my name — I'm the kind that scrolls past in the end credits.

This site was just for me at first. Some recordings, some photos, some days.

But after April 17, 2009, it became something else.

Xiaoman disappeared that day.

I don't know where she went. The police said they were looking, but nothing came of it.

Everything I know, everything I found, is here.

If you're reading this — it means I'm probably gone too.

Please find her.

— Mo Shen · April 17, 2009 · 23:47
