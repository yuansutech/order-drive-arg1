Name: Xiaoman Lin

Missing since: April 17, 2009

Last seen: South District, Qingchuan

Her laptop was found in her room; the hard drive had been formatted.

In her office drawer was a photo of the South District factory site, with the number 0417 written on the back.

That land was acquired by a real estate company in May 2009.

Case remains open.
